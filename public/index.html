01. The About Section

From Cultural Pastime to Official Competitive Discipline

The Ludo Academy of Excellence is the official training, educational, and accreditation arm of The Ludo League South Africa (LLSA). Founded in 2009 by President Joe Setladi, the LLSA has pioneered the transformation of Ludo from an informal household board game into a structured, nationally recognized competitive discipline.

Headquartered in Pretoria, Gauteng, with active regional hubs across Soweto, Alexandra, and Mamelodi, the Academy develops certified tournament players, official referees, and accredited talent agents across all nine provinces.

02. The Training Section

3-Day Intensive Accreditation Programme

The Ludo Academy offers a focused, practical 3-day training programme built for serious competitors, referees, and agency managers:

Day 1 — Foundation & Rules

In-depth analysis of official LLSA rules, dispute resolution frameworks, player ethics, and digital registration procedures.

Day 2 — Operations & Commerce

Logistics of tournament management, media visibility, commercial sponsorship integration, and talent identification.

Day 3 — Assessment & Accreditation

Practical case study evaluation, code of conduct verification, and the official awarding of national licences.

Skill Pathway

Three Levels of Mastery

Level 1 — Beginner Foundations

Master baseline strategic blockades, safety path navigation, and probability assessment based on standard dice mechanics.

Level 2 — Intermediate Tactics

Acquire racing trajectories, blockade manipulation, and double-token split configurations to control board pace.

Level 3 — Advanced Mastery

Dominate high-stakes tournament psychology, asset coordination, and defensive perimeter containment algorithms.

03. The Agents Section

Call for Official Ludo Agents

Ludo South Africa is actively inviting registered sports talent managers, agency representatives, and community entrepreneurs to become Official Ludo Agents. As the competitive circuit expands across the country, registered agents hold exclusive rights to scout talent, build player rosters, and represent athletes in official leagues and national championships.

Your Responsibilities as an Agent

  • Scout, recruit, and mentor talented Ludo players across township leagues.
  • Register your agency officially with Ludo South Africa and build a managed roster.
  • Manage commercial contracts, media appearance visibility, and endorsement opportunities.
  • Protect player interests and enforce strict compliance with the Official Code of Conduct.
