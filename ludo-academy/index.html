Professional Opportunity

Call for Ludo Agents

Become an Official Ludo South Africa Player Agency

Ludo South Africa is inviting registered agencies and sports talent representatives to become Official Ludo Agents. As the sport continues to grow across the country, this is an opportunity to establish your agency within a new and emerging professional sporting ecosystem from the very beginning.

Your Responsibilities

  • ▸Scout, recruit, and develop talented Ludo players.
  • ▸Register your agency with Ludo South Africa.
  • ▸Build and manage your own roster of players.
  • ▸Prepare players for leagues, tournaments, championships, and other official events.
  • ▸Work with Ludo South Africa to ensure players are available for competitions, media engagements, and commercial opportunities.
  • ▸Ensure all players are properly registered, accredited, and compliant with Ludo South Africa regulations.
  • ▸Guide and protect the professional interests of your players throughout their careers.

Why Become an Official Agent?

As one of the first officially recognised Ludo agencies in South Africa, you'll have the opportunity to establish relationships with players at the grassroots level and grow alongside the sport. Agencies that invest early will be well positioned as the player pool, competitions, sponsorships, and commercial opportunities expand over time.

Your registered players remain your agency's responsibility and portfolio. Whenever they earn prize money, secure sponsorships, endorsements, or other approved commercial deals, your agency will be entitled to its agreed share in accordance with Ludo South Africa regulations and player representation agreements.

Minimum Requirements

  • ✓A registered business with a valid licence to operate.
  • ✓Commitment to ethical player representation and development.
  • ✓Ability to recruit, manage, and support players professionally.
  • ✓Ensure every player under your agency is officially registered and accredited with Ludo South Africa.
  • ✓Comply with all Ludo South Africa rules, regulations, and the Official Agent Code of Conduct.

The Investment Perspective

The key is that agents should see the licence as an investment in a long-term profession, not just a participation fee. As the Ludo ecosystem grows — with more tournaments, sponsorships, media exposure, and player earnings — the value of holding an official licence will increase alongside it.

The professionalisation of Ludo is creating new opportunities beyond the game itself. Player representation will play an important role in shaping the future of the sport, and Ludo South Africa is looking for agencies ready to help build that future.
